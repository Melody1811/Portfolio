# Editing guidelines for Figma Make

Keep this buildless HTML/CSS/ES-module app and its interactions intact. Do not replace the spatial view with a static grid. Use the bundled local Three.js modules.

Preserve: desktop globe rotation and immersive navigation; mobile/tablet swipe/tap-only behavior at 1024px or coarse touch; 16 stable numeric project IDs; six editable Index case studies; light/dark mode; opening circle-to-globe animation; contact copy feedback; biography keyword image swaps; responsive dialogs; reduced-motion support; keyboard access.

Use a restrained editorial aesthetic: a fixed collection of credited, reuse-licensed neutral photography, restrained accents, ample whitespace, regular-weight system typography, and small quiet controls. Avoid live random-photo feeds, AI-generated imagery, or real client logos. This is a fictional sample portfolio.

Keep related data in sync across projects.js, media.js and case-studies.js. Replace all example.com contacts when personalizing. Do not restore personal content or hosting metadata from any source portfolio. Preserve third-party notices in vendor/.
